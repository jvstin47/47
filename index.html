Currently in my 2nd year pursuing a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering at Amal Jyothi College of Engineering (passing out in 2029), I architect and ship high-performance software at the convergence of stochastic simulation, edge intelligence, and tactile digital mechanics. Rather than wrapping surface-level APIs, I build from foundational physics and low-level constraints up—converting extreme operational chaos into deterministic, sub-millisecond execution.

My work ranges from autonomous search-and-rescue robotics with Phoenix X (SIH 26177)—running ThermalNet YOLOv8 edge inference at <20ms and 5km off-grid LoRa telemetry meshes on Qualcomm RB5 micro-UAVs—to spatial camera tracking systems (SIH 26169), vectorized Formula 1 race strategy engines evaluating 10,000 parallel futures, and decentralized health triage networks.

I treat engineering as a physical craft: whether optimizing NumPy linear algebra & PyTorch neural inference on constrained Qualcomm edge hardware, writing offline-first Android OS engines in Kotlin, or driving 60 FPS hardware-accelerated Canvas & WebGL pipelines. Resilient by default, zero bloat, and engineered for high stakes.
